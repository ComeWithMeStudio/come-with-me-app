# Come With Me 1.0

## Product idea
Come With Me is Mimi's little world: a child chooses where to go with Mimi instead of navigating a dry menu.

## Home screen
After the logo splash, Mimi welcomes the child at a crossroads with four destinations:

- **Рушаймо!** — пісні, рух, пригоди
- **Тихіше…** — колискові, спокій, перед сном
- **Розкажи мені** — казки та історії
- **Пограймо!** — ігри, букви, цифри, форми

The approved visual concept uses a sunny path for active adventures, a moonlit cozy area for quiet content, a book/story corner, a play/learning corner, and Mimi with a travel backpack in the center.

## Mimi
Mimi is the guide, not a menu category. Future interaction layer:
- blink / subtle idle motion
- react to taps
- look toward interface choices
- desktop cursor tracking
- mobile touch reactions

## Parent area
A small separate **Для дорослих** entry keeps settings, language, purchases/subscription and future progress information out of the child's main world.

## MVP flow
Splash → Home → one of four worlds → content selection → player → Home.

Initial version deliberately avoids accounts, complex profiles, points, stores and other heavy systems.

## Technical principles
- mobile-first and suitable for Telegram Mini App
- responsive web app
- prepare structure for Ukrainian / English
- keep monetization extensible without making payments part of the first build

## Wow-box / later
Interactive Mimi, rewards, personalization, progress, remembered position, easter eggs, time-of-day world states.
