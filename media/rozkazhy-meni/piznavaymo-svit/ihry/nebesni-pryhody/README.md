# Небесні пригоди Мімі

Five untimed activities based on the approved concept of 22 October 2026:

1. Choose the balloon, then raise it through three heights.
2. Choose the helicopter, then lift it vertically and hover over the pad.
3. Choose the red propeller airplane, then follow three cloud waypoints in order.
4. Choose the airship, then find a bridge, house and tree in any order.
5. Choose the passenger airplane, then fly to the illuminated night aerodrome.

Each chapter offers three choices; all five aircraft participate across the complete game. Wrong answers offer a gentle retry. Completion requires the child's action; chapters advance explicitly. The final screen includes all five aircraft and a restart.

Reuses the existing painted aircraft and day/night landscapes; rotor and propeller coordinates match the approved discovery. The airfield is CSS scenery; landmarks use emoji buttons. No generated bitmap assets were added.

Twelve dedicated Gigi MP3 clips now provide an introduction, choice and action prompts for each of the five adventures, and the finale. Settings remain Eleven Multilingual v2, stability .65, similarity .23, style .43 and speaker boost. All prompts also remain visible as text. Quiet daytime ambience (14%) and a soft completion chime are retained; night has no daytime ambience.

The extra blue river overlay is removed. The night target is labelled «Смуга аеродрому», keeps its dark runway surface after completion, and the aircraft finishes lower.
